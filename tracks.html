General Tracks

Pick a track and build something for the Shore!

Aerospace & Exploration

Reach for the stars! This track is all about the future of flight and the mysteries of our galaxy. Whether you're visualizing satellite data, building a launch tracker, or designing a mission control dashboard, show us how your code can help explore the final frontier and innovate for the next generation of space travel.

Community & Health

Innovating for our neighbors! Use your tech skills to bring world-class care and modern tools to our rural doorstep. From building platforms that help local students thrive to creating apps that link patients directly with healthcare providers, this track is for projects that put people first and make the Shore a better place to live.

Cybersecurity

Build digital trust! Help create a resilient future for the Shore’s most vital networks. Whether you're securing local banking tools, protecting community healthcare data, or shielding the farmers and small businesses that drive our region, show us how you can keep our home safe, secure, and strong against modern threats.
